Working in Groups or Alone

Nowadays, many teacher ask students to finish project in groups. Some people think study alone is better, but I believe that it is better for students to work in groups than alone, because group work can make student learn from each other and practice communication skill.

First, working in group can let students learn from each other. For example, last semester my biology class did a project about the river near our school. I was not good at drawing graph, but my classmate Lily taught me how to use Excel to make chart. If I study alone, I will never learn this. So group work is more useful.

Second, group work can practice communication skill. In the future job, everyone need to work with other people. When we discuss in group, we must explain our idea clearly and listen others. This is very important for our future.

Of course, some people say group work is not fair, because some student do nothing. But teacher can give each member different task to solve this problem.

In conclusion, group work is more better than study alone. Students should work in groups more often.
