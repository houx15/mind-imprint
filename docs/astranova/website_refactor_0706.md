## 1. Mission

Per Aspera exists to raise the true masters of the AI era. As AI makes knowledge output nearly free, the abilities that matter shift to what AI cannot replace: deep critical reasoning, and the product thinking that turns problems into solutions. We build the product and the services that teach both — and, just as importantly, **measure** both.

## 2. Why now

- **Companies are bypassing college.** Palantir's Meritocracy Fellowship drew 500+ high-school graduates for 22 paid seats ($5,400/month) with the slogan "Skip the debt." AI is consuming junior roles, so companies are moving talent development earlier.
- **Policy is forcing schools to change.** China's Ministry of Education now requires AI courses across K-12, with teacher training mandated and industry experts invited into schools. Similar mandates are spreading globally. Procurement demand is being created directly by policy.
- **AI-era schools are already working.** Astra Nova (born inside SpaceX) opens its high school in August 2026 — admissions with no transcripts and no test scores, judged only on how a student reasons. Alpha School operates 13 campuses at tuitions up to $75k/year.

The structural logic: AI compresses junior roles → companies move training earlier → schools must import real problems and real assessment → yet no ready-made system exists for them to buy. Every proven education franchise (Montessori/AMI, PBL/PBLWorks, IB) now needs an AI upgrade — that increment is our market.

## 3. The core product: Mind Imprint

Mind Imprint (思维印记) is one integrated AI education product with three components. Human services layer on top of the product — never the other way around.

### 3.1 AI-assisted interactive courses

No recorded video lectures — passive video is a pre-AI format. Courses are live, small-group and AI-assisted, built on two threads: critical thinking and product thinking. The critical-thinking thread is grounded in 30+ courses developed and proven in real international classrooms (Venice overtourism, fossil-fuel funding-chain tracing, climate misinformation analysis, and more).

### 3.2 AI-guided workbenches (×2)

- **Critical Thinking Workbench:** interactive tasks in questioning, source verification, argument construction and revision — the AI guides, probes and challenges, but never concludes for the child.
- **Product Thinking Workbench:** real, age-adapted problems from our corporate network, worked from problem statement to shipped prototype, with AI as a colleague whose output must be verified.

### 3.3 Process assessment

A 9-dimension rubric × L1–L4 levels × an initiative gradient (self-initiated / prompted / absent) turns the reasoning process itself into a report card. Every interaction inside courses and workbenches is assessment data. Design red lines: the AI only asks, it never thinks for the child; no addictive mechanics, ever. A validation study (LLM scoring vs. blind ratings by senior IB examiners, weighted κ ≥ 0.7, on real pilot-school data) is scheduled for Q1 2027 and will be published.

## 6. Why we win

- **Assessment is the moat.** History shows pedagogy scales when assessment is built in (IB) and stalls when it is not (PBL). We are building the assessment layer for AI-era abilities — as IB's assessment is to international curricula.
- **A real-problem supply chain.** Corporate problems from the founders' startup network, redesigned for age, delivered by student teams, fed back by companies into each child's Mind Imprint record. Each piece exists somewhere; the interlock exists nowhere else.
- **An antifragile position.** Every time models get stronger, AI tools converge and commoditize, the half AI cannot replace grows scarcer, and demand for process assessment hardens. Our assets appreciate with the models.
- **Classroom-grown, product-delivered.** The curriculum was grown over years in real IB classrooms; the product ships it with AI at software economics — education depth × product speed.
