 the guide journey in my imagination:

  when a new user comes, we firstly pop up a modal, welcome him (xx你好呀，欢迎来到思维印记AI思辨力成长平台。我是印记，你的AI小伙伴。要不让我先带你逛逛这里吧！想先看看什么呢？) with options: courses/projects/ no, maybe later(which will tell the user, you can call the journey later at xxx - maybe an icon at the bottom of the nav bar, under which is a feedback button, and a logout button)
  so if user selects courses/projects, we begin courses/projects guide first, then the other one.

  courses guide, would firstly introduce why we have these courses (印记认为，思辨力是AI时代最重要的能力，而这一能力可以通过系统化的学习和操练提升，因此，我们准备了xxx的课程)

  then highlights (with the other parts shaded) the course categories, introduce that we have these categories.

  then guides students to enter one course.

  then introduces our AI-guided course journey, teaches students how to interact in the course.

  then show an example course report, introduces the components in the course report.

  then guides students they can also switch to course reports page to see the reports.

  then guides students to the tool card page and introduces our tool cards.
  and guide students to click one card to see the content of the cards. then guide students to show the practice history and see the relevant course.

  we first introduces the idea of AI-guided writing projects, w its borderline, and has evaluation.

  then guide students to open the demo project.

  then first highlights the top 立题-管理-阅读-写作-回顾 bar to five parts of a project. (and by default the demo projectlands at the 立题 page).

  then introduces the 立题 stage: 1) what components we need to talk about during this stage. 2) highlight sidebar, and tell that AI would guide you to think about these components. (and show AI's several components: ask questions, 提问卡, hint to let you write this to the 立题form).

  then introduces the 管理 part, 1) introduces 甘特图; 2) introduces 甘特图 can be edited through AI or manually and exported; 3) introduces kanban;
  4) introduces activity log, show that it can be auto added, aroduces it can also be exported.

  search, click one paper to view.)
