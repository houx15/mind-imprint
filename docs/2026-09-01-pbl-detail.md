### from question to answer plan

Students can begin with an idea, a vague interest, or nothing at all. If students don't have explitic idea or question, The AI first explains how to generate question from observation, with a simple observation method and gives them a small real-world mission. Students return with information, maybe a photo, voice note, drawing, or short paragraph. or students described some thoughts and begin brainstorm.

we have sticky board where we can manage sticky notes—observations, quotes, assumptions, and questions—which students can correct, add to, and organize. and use when necessary, like brainstorming, or breakdown students' observations.

Patterns in the board or students' feedback reveal several possible problem threads. Students choose one to investigate, gather more evidence, and **reframe** it into a clear “Who needs what, and why?” statement and then a “How might we?” question. They then brainstorm many solutions with the AI, combine and compare ideas, choose a promising next experiment, and update the project plan. during this process, AI guides them along the "who needs what and why" question, and "how might we" question. from observation to question to possible solution.

The process is iterative: new observations can change the problem, solution, and plan at any time, with every major update reviewed and confirmed by the student.

### reviewer: review AI's output

A good review, the first requirement is a easy-to-read thing needs my review.

we may ask students to review a document(like the design of a product-PRD, or an architecture document of a product coding), an output image (generated image), a built website.

for documents, good thing to review, is highlighted sentences with questions to answer, explanations for each part so that we know what we should care about in each part. Click on any word to have a new chat line with AI to know why and what.

for image and website, they are easy to read.

but for all materials, we can have a review sugestion, to ask students to answer several dimensions that reviewing this material must pay attention to.
